Challenge

Local businesses were quiet rooms.

Coming out of COVID, independent restaurant and bar owners across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex had lost a large share of their regular customer base and had no reliable way to bring people back through the door. Most had no in-house marketing function, no events pipeline, and no bandwidth to build either while trying to keep the business running.

They didn't need a consultant with a slide deck. They needed someone to own the whole night — the concept, the room, the staffing, the guest list, and the follow-through — from a first walkthrough of an empty venue to a line out the door.

Approach

One partner, every piece of the night.

Founded MCN Group as a mission-driven networking company built on a simple trade: local owners get a fully produced, professionally run event designed to put their space in front of new customers, and guests get a genuinely curated experience with real networking value, not just another generic promo.

Scout the venue. Design the theme. Run the room.

Every partnership started the same way: scouting and securing a venue, consulting with the owner on their media presence, then building a themed event from the ground up (a Thanksgiving dinner, a seasonal celebration, a brand or menu promotion) around that specific room and audience, all designed to drive foot traffic and sales.

What I Did

Four disciplines, one founder.

Creative Vision & Brand · Founder, MCN Group

Owned the full creative direction for every event: the theme (a holiday dinner, a seasonal celebration, a brand or menu promotion), the menu concepts developed with each venue partner, and the guest walkthrough, treating the path from entry to the main activation the same way as a CX journey map. Designed the event posters and led all media and social assets end to end.

Venue & Vendor Relations

Scouted and secured venue partnerships across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, including Mighty Chick Pocha, VIBE Lounge, and Mad for Chicken, and owned the vendor relationships (menu and beverage programs, sound and lighting, promotional talent) behind every event.

Staffing & Community Partnerships

Built and managed on-site event staffing, including bartenders, local security, and off-duty police, and partnered with local community and promotion groups, including Asian Friends of DFW, to build guest lists and extend each event's reach into new audiences.

Team Leadership & Flagship Build-Out

Led a core team of 4, plus contracted design and media talent, across 6 events ranging from 60 to 550 guests. For the flagship event, owned the full venue build-out — transforming the physical space, producing print posters, running marketing and the guest list, and hiring promotional recruiters.

Results

Six nights, zero blueprint.

In under a year and a half, grew MCN Group from a first venue walkthrough into 6 fully produced events across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, with guest counts ranging from an initial 60-guest activation up to a 550-guest flagship, each one designed, staffed, and run by a founder-led team of 4 plus contracted creative talent.

Every venue partner got a full night of production they didn't have the time or infrastructure to build themselves: a designed experience, a managed guest list, and a room that felt intentional from the moment guests walked in — the same relationship-first, detail-first approach that carried into every stakeholder relationship since.

Key Learning

Hospitality and CX are the same discipline in a different room. Every lever that builds trust in a digital product — a clear path through the experience, a consistent brand voice, staffing that shows up right — builds a great event, too. Owning a room end to end, with a team of four to lean on, is what taught me to design an experience path long before I ever called it a journey map.
