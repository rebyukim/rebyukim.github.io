Challenge

Same skill. Four completely different rooms.

Four organizations, four totally different definitions of "stakeholder": a federally funded research program spanning 12+ partner institutions, an agency-side pitch into a global electronics brand that hadn't agreed to build anything yet, an all-volunteer nonprofit media team with no infrastructure or process connecting the client side to the creative team pipeline, and a Fortune 500 company's day-to-day B2C/B2B web operation.

Each one handed me a new system, a new vocabulary, and stakeholders who needed answers yesterday. Every time, the underlying job was the same: master the system, earn the client's trust, and elevate the work.

Approach

One playbook. Recalibrated each time.

Regardless of the room, the method stays the same: understand what this specific audience actually needs before proposing anything, translate technical or data-heavy work into language they can act on, keep a communication cadence steady enough that people trust you before there's a problem, and follow through on the small things, because that's what the big trust is actually built from.

The stakeholders change. The relationship-first method doesn't.

What I Did

Four organizations, one throughline.

SMU / UCLA · Project Specialist

Owned operational execution for a $17M NIMH-funded, multi-site clinical research program with UCLA, serving as the primary point of contact for 12+ partner institutions on budget, timelines, and milestone delivery. Redesigned the participant experience journey using site-level data, reducing dropout by 20%.

LG Electronics · SJ Design Studio → HSAD · Digital Strategist → Program Manager, CX Innovation

Pitched LG SimRoom, a first-of-its-kind virtual TV showroom, while on the agency side at SJ Design Studio, before LG had agreed to build it. Once greenlit, was scouted directly onto the account at HSAD to own its adoption, scaling the platform until it resolved 85 to 90 percent of the support interactions it was built for, while aligning Engineering, Data Science, and regional marketing around a shared post-purchase strategy that generated 1.5M personalized communications monthly and a 10-point NPS improvement.

Onnuri English Ministry · Community Media & Operations Lead

Built trust with an all-volunteer team and a community audience, with no infrastructure or process connecting the client side to the creative team pipeline. Mentored contributors, set a sustainable content cadence, and used engagement data to guide creative direction, doubling the team's output capacity while growing organic reach by 113%.

Samsung Electronics America · via Cheil USA · Project Manager, Digital Experience

Recruited to lead Samsung's transition from US-only systems to global JIRA and AEM platforms, building a 24/7 project governance framework across 5 product divisions. Serve as Cheil's day-to-day client contact for Samsung's Visual Display business, owning SLA performance and stakeholder alignment across the B2C/B2B Samsung.com ecosystem as the primary liaison between Marketing, CX, Creative, Product, QA, and Dev.

Results

The range is the proof point.

A federally funded research consortium, an agency-side pitch, an all-volunteer nonprofit, and a Fortune 500 e-commerce operation don't have much in common on paper. But each one responded to the same approach: understand the audience first, translate the complexity, show up consistently, and let the results speak.

That's the muscle I bring to any client or stakeholder relationship: the ability to read a room I've never worked in before, figure out what "trust" looks like there, and earn it fast enough to move the work forward.
